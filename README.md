# Executive Activity, Revenue & Customer Servicing Intelligence Framework

A Business Analysis case study: a centralized "master input" framework that gave managers one daily view of customer coverage, meeting activity, servicing priorities and revenue opportunities, and the evidence behind revenue commitments.

> **Status:** Ongoing (started June 2025) | **Role:** Business Analyst | **Version:** 1.1
> All company, customer, employee and revenue data has been removed or anonymized. Terminology is generalized.

## The Problem
Renewal reporting showed meetings and prospects, but not *why* a customer was targeted, how often they had been met, or whether they needed servicing or revenue focus. Revenue commitments also lacked consistent supporting context.

## The Solution
A consolidated master input deck that:
- captures daily field activity through a Google Form into a Google Sheet
- consolidates engagement, payment, meeting and product-category data
- segments customers for servicing and revenue focus
- flags potential upgrade opportunities using multiple indicators (not a single metric)
- gives managers the context to prioritize meetings and explain revenue commitments

## Outcomes
- All Team Managers adopted the master input for daily review
- Managers could direct Field Executives on where and why to focus meetings
- Revenue commitments could be explained with customer and opportunity context
- Renewal rates showed an increasing trend after implementation *(observed trend; causality not established)*
